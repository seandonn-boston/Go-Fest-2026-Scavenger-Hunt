How it works
  1. You hunt both event days — a fresh set of 4 tasks each day, drawn from that day’s habitats: Catch 26, Obtain 1 shiny (catching or trading both count), Catch 1 Mewtwo, and High five your Community Ambassador.
  2. Evolutions count! A “Catch 26 Pikachu” task is completed by any mix of Pichu, Pikachu, or Raichu (any form).
  3. The Catch and Shiny tasks can each be rerolled once per day — two rerolls a day, four for the weekend. You’ll never be dealt the same species twice.
  4. Done? Show this screen and your game to a Community Ambassador to claim your reward. Nothing to submit, nothing to connect.
